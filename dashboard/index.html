Recovery operations

Every failed payment is diagnosed, gated by a deterministic policy engine the model cannot bypass, and either acted on within bounded limits or stopped with a recorded reason.

What this is

A downtime-aware recovery agent for failed payments: an AI diagnosis layer proposes a recoverability class and action, a deterministic policy engine authorizes it against fixed rules the model cannot see or bypass, and only an authorized verdict ever reaches an executor. The differentiating claim isn't the AI — it's that recovered value is measured against a randomized holdout arm, not reported as a gross total the model could take credit for regardless of whether it helped.
